# Music credits

Each CD in the game plays a real song under a Creative Commons Attribution licence. We cut a short
section of each (no re-encoding) and filter it in the game: muffled and mono in 2004, clean today.
Licences were checked on each track's own page.

- **Mira, "first songs"** (`mira.mp3`, 40 s from 2:59): "Deflated & Alone (acoustic)" by The.Spirit.Of.Light,
  vocals by Kaer Trouz, https://ccmixter.org/files/thespiritoflight/19354, CC BY 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Trimmed and filtered for this game.
- **The Static, "Feedback Summer"** (`static.mp3`, 40 s from 1:11): "Teenager" by The Crypts!
  (https://freemusicarchive.org/music/The_Crypts_1935/No_Life_After_Crypts_1347/The_Crypts_-_01_-_Teenagermp3_1210/),
  CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Trimmed and filtered for this game.
- **DJ K-Bit, "Polyphonic"** (`kbit.mp3`, 24 s from 0:48): "Enthusiast" by Tours
  (https://freemusicarchive.org/music/Tours/Enthusiast/Tours_-_Enthusiast/), CC BY 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Trimmed and filtered for this game.

These are the ★ picks from `docs/music-shortlist.md`, used because the team had not picked yet.
Anyone can swap a file: keep the name, update this list and `T.audio.music.cds` in `src/tuning.ts`.

## The songs the CDs cover (not in this repo)

The story's CDs are local covers of "Lose Yourself" (Eminem, 2002), "Sweet Child O' Mine" (Guns N'
Roses, 1987) and "Despacito" (Luis Fonsi ft. Daddy Yankee, 2017). Those recordings are copyrighted
and are never committed or deployed: the files above stand in for them. For a local demo you can put
your own copies in `src/audio/local-songs/` (git-ignored; see the README there).
